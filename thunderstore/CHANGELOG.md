# Changelog

## 0.1.167 — Spell, targeting and compatibility fixes

### English

Special thanks to **Rratvar, the Untitled** and **Brandon** for their bug reports, investigations, and technical advice.

- **Arcane Slay** also triggers when a spell kills a friendly unit.
- **Triple Impact Exam** can target friendly units.
- **Interdepartmental Damage Memo:** each copy adds one use, and each eligible spell spends one use. Repetitions do not spend additional uses.
- Repeated spells retain their own **Trample**, **Explosive** and other damage traits. **Enchanted Dissertation** preserves these traits alongside its friendly and enemy targeting.
- Cross-floor repetitions use the destination floor’s targeting rules, including boss filters, and restore the original camera view after the sequence.
- **W&W spell Trample** carries excess damage through front enemies until exhausted, including after hitting a friendly unit or a rear enemy. Transferred damage respects mitigation and is not multiplied again by Spell Weakness.
- **Extra Credit** retains its own spell traits while assigning kill credit to its creator. Saving that attribution from the map no longer accesses combat-only managers.
- **Credit Conversion** and card selections resolve the events generated by their effects before returning control.
- Closing a hand selection clears lingering **Not Eligible** labels and darkened cards.
- Fixed missing descriptions for granted states and traits, including **Conduit** and **Purge**.
- Added **Frozen Enrollment Scholarship**’s activation message and **Extended Exam Session**’s per-turn use counter.
- **Desk Swap** respects movement restrictions and triggers native movement effects after completing the swap. Its immediate attack uses the original rear unit only if it survives and remains at the front.
- Improved preview handling to avoid unavailable battle managers and changes to live units whose temporary states have not been prepared. Cross-floor preview expansion is limited to relevant W&W effects.
- Improved compatibility with **Conductor** hit and discard triggers. Arcane Slay, Overheal and Overdamage avoid dispatching custom events to units without their respective triggers.
- Reworked effect-duration and relic handling while preserving existing durations and relic IDs.
- Added diagnostic information for runs that cannot resolve their saved main clan.

## 0.1.164 — Massive Update

### English

Massive update! I’ve probably squashed 30 bugs and created 31 new ones.

#### New starting grafts

Five new illustrated grafts, attached to their starting units and excluded from normal reward pools:

| Graft | Effect | Starting unit |
|---|---|---|

- Postdoc’s innate Undying moves to its amulet, preserving one charge.
- All five grafts have full-background card illustrations and separate transparent equipment icons.
- Training Wand displays its current Conduit without duplicate or conflicting values.

#### Card changes

- **Triple Impact Exam:** increased damage from 1 to **4 per hit**, three hits.
- **Exam Week:** gains **Distinct**, preventing it from drawing copies of itself.
- **Vital Records Exchange:** also restores **4 health to both affected units** after swapping their health.
- **Consumable Credits Intern:** joins banner rewards in place of Professor of Shared Damages. Automatically selects the only eligible spell in hand.
- **Armored Honors Enrollment:** its zero-Attack effect expires after the next combat round, including during Relentless.
- **Dissertation with Major Revisions:** can target friendly units.
- **Professor of Magical Education:** rounds half the front ally’s Attack up. Displayed spell values and resolution use the same calculation.
- Adjusted several base Attack values to even numbers, including the Dean’s nominal path values of **6/10/16**.
- **One Turn Sabbatical:** preserves its healing bonus from the moment it is cast until the delayed healing resolves. Its friendly-target restriction is clearer.

#### Enchanted Dissertation

- Excludes spells with random targets.
- Excludes spells that open selection dialogs during combat.
- Invalid choices are disabled and display **Not Eligible**.
- Accepts supported targetless gold, Dragon’s Hoard and draw effects, including **Alchemy**.
- Its marker displays each bound spell’s full description, remaining attacks and target side.
- Rechecks eligibility before binding and casting.
- Preserves beneficial, harmful, ambiguous and area-targeting behavior. Bound spells return to the discard pile when the effect ends.

#### Combat and interaction fixes

- **Lightning Rod Intern:** previews no longer refund real Ember. Predicted damage and Dazed use temporary character states.
- Ally-only spells such as **Immortal Trade** no longer trigger its redirection penalties.
- **Course Withdrawal:** correctly resolves **discard → Ember refund → draw**.
- **Conduit Credit Transfer:** its lasting Explosive grant survives the expiration of Explosive Requisition’s next-use grant.
- **First Spell Class Representative:** counts damage to Armor and overkill across all floors. Damage Shield still blocks damage.
- **Extra Credit:** recognized by Conductor’s damage-spell filters for shop upgrades.
- **Spatial Credit Transfer:** corrected the negative size notification.
- Suppressed champion-aura status popups during prediction.
- Clarified PhD’s health-loss accounting and Arcane Liability Insurance’s trigger tooltip. PhD retains its equal healing split among all allies.

#### Out-of-combat tooltip hotfix

- Fixed card-text patches accessing combat-only managers while browsing the deck outside battle, causing repeated assertions and excessive log growth.
- Added availability checks to Education, Pair Assignment, Awkward Silence, Strictly Platonic and dynamic card refreshes.
- These patches now use the current game context instead of retaining managers from a previous battle.

#### Tooltips and presentation

- Added native explanations for generated cards and granted keywords, including **Extra Credit, Attuned, Explosive, Ephemeral, Stasis, Armor, Dazed and Conduit**.
- Added protection against missing tooltip identifiers and diagnostic logging to identify the affected W&W card if a tooltip fails.
- **Awkward Silence:** synchronized its card and in-play room descriptions.
- Clan banners use the native silhouette and hover presentation with a W&W emblem.
- Updated affected text across all ten supported languages.

#### Internal improvements

- Arcane Slay now uses **Conductor’s hit-event registry and queue**. Rewards resolve on each killing hit.
- Reused native attack, selection, upgrade and relic-condition components.
- Separated active card effects, shared selectors and unit passives from the general combat runtime.
- Isolated retired-card compatibility code.
- Separated shared trigger handling from clan-specific effects.

#### Requirements

- Requires **Conductor 0.5.16**.

Huge thanks to **Rratvar, the Untitled** and **Brandon** for testing, bug reports and detailed technical advice!
